## Why Live?

Modern smartphones are designed to maximize screen time through constant visual stimulation, notification badges, and frictionless app drawers that encourage compulsive checking.

Live is designed to break this cycle through **intentional friction**:
- **Apps hidden by default**: Your home screen only shows what you need to stay grounded: the time, date, local weather, and direct access to essential communication.
- **16-Digit Passcode Gate**: On first launch, you configure a 16-digit passcode. Every time you want to open the full app list or change launcher settings, you must enter this passcode. This deliberate pause eliminates mindless scrolling and helps you regain control over your attention.
- **Three-Screen Minimalist Flow**:
  - **Focus Home**: Clean typography with an oversized clock, date, live temperature, direct dial shortcuts, and essential communication apps (Phone, Messages, Gmail).
  - **Calendar and Holidays**: Full interactive month grid, year picker, offline holiday tracker, and selected date detail view.
  - **Pinned Essentials**: Whitelist your most important apps for direct access while keeping distractions hidden.
- **Quick Contacts Speed Dial**: Reorderable direct-call shortcuts with smooth drag-and-drop handles and contextual menus.
- **Zero-Noise Weather**: Lightweight real-time weather and temperature updates powered by Open-Meteo with background caching.
- **Searchable App Drawer**: Fast, clutter-free search across all installed applications, accessible only when unlocked.

## How to Use

1. Download the latest APK from the [Releases](https://github.com/localhost969/live/releases) page.
2. Install the APK and set Live as your default home launcher.
3. Configure your 16-digit passcode on first launch and start using.
